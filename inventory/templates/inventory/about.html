How the AI works (and why it is here)

Two techniques - NLP and RAG - are built into StockWise. Both run 100% inside Python: no external API, no data leaving your MySQL database, no per-query cost.

1. NLP - natural language questions & smart matching

Where: the Assistant page (inventory/nlp/intent.py) and CSV import (inventory/services/csvio.py).

How: your free-text question is classified into an intent (low-stock check, item quantity, category listing, overview, help) using a weighted pattern classifier, and entities (which item? which category?) are extracted with fuzzy string matching. With the intent and entities the app answers from live MySQL data - exact numbers, exact items. The same fuzzy matcher guards CSV imports: an incoming row that is 87%+ similar to an existing item is flagged as a duplicate instead of silently doubling your stock.

Meaning for you: warehouse staff never learn query syntax - they just ask. Benefit: faster answers on the floor, fewer import mistakes, and every question is logged (QueryLog) so you can see what the team actually wants to know.

2. RAG - retrieval-augmented answers

Where: inventory/rag/ (index, retriever, generator), used whenever a question does not match a known intent - e.g. “who supplies the laser toner and where is it stored?”

How: each active item becomes a document bundling its facts (name, SKU, category, supplier, location, quantity, threshold, status, notes). A TF-IDF vector-space index is built in-process; the question is vectorised the same way; the top-k most similar records are retrieved; the answer is generated strictly from those retrieved records and shown with its sources. An optional OpenAI-compatible LLM can refine the wording (set RAG_LLM_ENABLED=1) - but retrieval and grounding stay identical, and the offline synthesiser is always the fallback.

Meaning for you: you can ask questions no one pre-programmed. Benefit: answers are grounded in your real records with visible sources - the model cannot invent stock levels - and because retrieval happens in-process over MySQL data, sensitive inventory details never leave your server.

3. NLG - the morning briefing

Where: inventory/nlp/summary.py, shown on the dashboard and printed by python manage.py check_stock.

How: the raw counters (healthy / low / out, biggest shortfalls, total value) are turned into a short readable paragraph. Deterministic templates mean the same data always produces the same sentence - auditable and dependency-free.

Meaning for you: one glance instead of one table. Benefit: managers get a status sentence in email or console before the first coffee; the client gets fewer stock-outs.

Why no external AI service by default?

Inventory data is commercially sensitive and every external call costs money and adds latency plus a failure mode. The default pipeline is deterministic, offline, free per query, and identical on macOS, Windows and Linux. When you want more fluent wording, flip on the optional LLM hook - the grounding, sources and audit trail stay the same.
